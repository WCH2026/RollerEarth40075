01Singapore Book of Records · 11–12 March 2026
203.79km in 24 h

Longest Round Singapore Inline Skating in 24 Hours

On 11–12 March 2026, Konstantin Lyuik and Dmitry Parfentyev skated 203.79 km in 24 hours and set a new Singapore record, at an average speed of 12.2 km/h.

This was not a track attempt. The whole route was skated in a real urban environment across Singapore, on public roads, park connectors and industrial areas as far as Tuas Lamp Post 1, which made the challenge significantly more demanding.

The certificate and the Singapore Book of Records were presented by Mr Ong Eng Huat at a ceremony at Merlion Park, in the presence of friends, supporters and members of the skating community. The distance and average speed were monitored with Strava.
